Our Core Philosophy & Caring Approach

Every child is unique. We nurture curious minds, encourage natural creativity, and foster strong social foundations through play.

Nurturing Home Atmosphere

A secure and comfortable home setting where children feel safe, loved, and valued as individuals. Small group sizes ensure dedicated personal attention for your little one.

Play-Based Learning & EYLF

Following the Early Years Learning Framework (EYLF), our curriculum uses hands-on play, outdoor activity, art, and music to stimulate early literacy and social skills.

Administrative & Fee Policies

Operating strictly under the Community Vision Family Daycare Scheme guidelines.

Approved Scheme Affiliation

Nipona's Family Daycare works directly with Community Vision Family Daycare. Community Vision manages scheme compliance, Australian Child Care Subsidy (CCS) processing, and regular quality assurance audits.

Scheme Partner Community Vision

Invoicing & Payment Schedule

  • Wednesday: Statements and invoices are emailed directly to parents showing care hours and applied subsidies.
  • Thursday: Outstanding gap payment is automatically collected via Direct Debit (mandatory upon enrollment).
Automated, predictable weekly processing.

No Direct Cash Policy

Important Policy: Educators are strictly not permitted to collect cash or payments directly from parents. All financial transactions must proceed through the official Community Vision direct debit system.
